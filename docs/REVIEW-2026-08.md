# NeuralCard: design review, August 2026

Reviewed at `main` (`6a64aac`), working tree clean. Board `hardware/NeuralCard.kicad_pcb`:
2 layers, 0.8 mm, 85.6 × 53.98 mm, 57 footprints, fully SMT, ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 + LSM6DS3TR-C
IMU + ST25DV04KC NFC tag + etched NFC coil + 24 charlieplexed LEDs + CR2032.

Tools: KiCad 10.0.3 (`kicad-cli`). Every measurement below was taken from the board file,
the project file, the libraries, or a tool run, not inferred. Nothing was changed.

**Verdict: blocked.** Four defects prevent the product working as designed. Several more
would fail a fab or assembly review. The board currently reports `Found 0 violations` because
the checks that would report them are switched off or set against a threshold of zero.

---

## Root cause: the design is not datasheet-driven

This is the finding that explains most of the others.

The repo holds **one datasheet, `hardware/datasheets/ESP32-S3-WROOM-1.pdf`, for thirteen
distinct footprints.** Eleven of the thirteen are unverified `easyeda2kicad` downloads, still
in KiCad-5 `(module …)` syntax with v6 fragments spliced in by hand. They have never been
round-tripped through the footprint editor, and their defects are consistent with
auto-generation rather than transcription from a mechanical drawing:

- **No `F.Fab` geometry on 11 of 13 footprints.** `F.Fab` *is* the datasheet body outline.
  Its absence is direct evidence nobody transcribed one.
- **Every courtyard wraps the body and ignores the pads**, an auto-generated shape, not a
  measured one.
- **Pin-1 "markers" are Ø0.12 mm origin artifacts** at 0.06 mm line width, below any silk
  minimum. They will not print.
- **`(attr through_hole)` on two all-SMD footprints**, nobody checked what the part is.

And the one datasheet that *is* held specifies the requirement the board's worst defect
violates: the ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 sheet shows a **"Keepout Zone"** in its pin layout (§3.1) and a
**7.49 mm "Antenna Area"** in the module dimensions. That zone is 84% copper on this board.

The one footprint on the board that passes every check, **SW3**, got there by referencing
KiCad's shipped `SW_SPDT_Shouhan_MSK12C02` unmodified. Not by transcribing a datasheet either,
but by trusting a library that already had.

## BLOCKER: the product does not work as designed

### B1. The ESP32 antenna keep-out is flooded with ground copper

Espressif's mandatory clearance zone, in board coordinates **x 23.0 to 41.0, y 37.36 to 43.39**
(the pad-free module end, marked by the footprint's own `B.SilkS` boundary lines), measures:

| Layer | Copper coverage |
|---|---|
| F.Cu | **84.4 %** |
| B.Cu | **81.3 %** |

There is no keep-out zone anywhere near it. The requirement is stated in the datasheet in this
repo. This alone prevents the WiFi working to spec, and it is not recoverable in firmware.

**Fix:** add a keep-out over the antenna area on *both* copper layers, Espressif's own
guidance is all-layer, and re-pour. Confirm against §3.1 and Figure 10.1 of the module
datasheet rather than against the silk outline.

### B2. Five ground islands are electrically isolated from the main plane

GND has **no tracks at all** on this board; the 28 GND vias are the only link between the
F.Cu and B.Cu pours. Five groups are stranded:

| Isolated region | Area | Pads stranded on it |
|---|---|---|
| F.Cu 4.47 to 27.53 × 4.83 to 10.47 + B.Cu 21.31 to 24.32 × 7.05 to 9.47 | 73.97 mm² | **U2.6, U2.7: the IMU's only ground pins** |
| B.Cu 21.39 to 26.63 × 4.66 to 18.06 | 22.40 mm² | C1.2, C5.2 |
| B.Cu 6.60 to 8.42 × 4.26 to 8.81 | 3.93 mm² | **U4.4: the ST25DV's only ground pin** |
| B.Cu 10.36 to 11.20 × 4.82 to 8.03 | 2.42 mm² | C11.2 |
| B.Cu 21.39 to 22.24 × 20.83 to 21.92 | 0.76 mm² | C2.2 |

The IMU and the NFC tag have no ground return. Four of the five 100 nF local decouplers have
their ground ends floating.

`kicad-cli pcb drc --severity-all` already reports these as **5 unconnected items**. Commit
`bf5ac61 "fix: pour islands cleared"` did not clear them. Island removal did not catch them
because each island touches a pad of the net, a clean island-removal result is not evidence
of zero floating copper.

**Fix:** stitch each region to the main plane, or reroute so the pour is continuous. Re-run
DRC and require zero unconnected items before release.

### B3. The ESP32 has no decoupling capacitor

U1 has a **single power pin** (pad 2, `+3V3`, at 23.250, 34.620). Measured distance to the
nearest same-net capacitor:

| Cap | Distance | Approx. trace inductance at Ott's 10 nH/in |
|---|---|---|
| C2 (100 nF) | **13.46 mm** | ~5.3 nH |
| C1 (100 nF) | 18.37 mm | ~7.2 nH |
| C8 (10 µF bulk) | **21.80 mm** | ~8.6 nH |

Nothing is placed against pad 2. For comparison, U2 (the IMU) has C5 at 2.10 mm, so the
practice exists on this board, it just was not applied to the part that needs it most: the
fastest, highest-current device and a 2.4 GHz transmitter.

Bulk capacitors C9/C10 sit 25.8 mm from where power actually enters at SW3.

**Fix:** a 100 nF as close to pad 2 as placement allows, on the same side, with its ground via
adjacent rather than at the end of a stub.

### B4. A CR2032 cannot run an ESP32-S3 with WiFi

Transmit peaks around 350 mA against a cell rated roughly 3 mA continuous and 15 mA pulse.
The 132 µF of bulk MLCC on the board does not bridge that, and it sits 26 mm from the switch.

This is a power-architecture problem, not a layout problem, and it sits upstream of everything
else in this document. Options are a different cell chemistry, a supercapacitor across the
rail, or accepting that the radio only runs on external power.

## HIGH: will cause a rejection, a wrong build, or a silent failure

### H1. DRC is checking against zero

`hardware/NeuralCard.kicad_pro`, `board.design_settings.rules`:

```
min_clearance: 0.0        min_track_width: 0.0     min_connection: 0.0
min_silk_clearance: 0.0   solder_mask_to_copper_clearance: 0.0
```

`clearance` and `track_width` are both at severity **error** and *cannot fire*, because the
constraint they check against is zero. No fab process window was ever entered into Board Setup.

This is more dangerous than a rule set to Ignore: an audit of severities reports it compliant.
The CHANGELOG's "DRC 0 clearance violations" is therefore not a result.

**Fix:** enter JLCPCB's published minimums for the ordered class into Board Setup → Constraints,
then re-run.

### H2. 29 of 57 placements reference a 3D model path that does not exist

Every `easyeda2kicad` footprint hard-codes an absolute path:

```
/absolute/path/to/project/JLC.3dshapes/LED0603_L1.6-W0.8-H0.6.wrl
```

That directory does not exist. Meanwhile `hardware/JLC.3dshapes/` holds matching `.step` **and**
`.wrl` files for all 11 parts, and `${KIPRJMOD}` is already used correctly in `fp-lib-table`.

Affected: 24 LEDs + 2 tact switches + BT1 + U1 + U2. Nothing in KiCad complains, a wrong or
missing 3D model produces no DRC error and no electrical consequence, which is why it survived.
It breaks any mechanical fit check, exploded diagram, or enclosure work downstream.

**Fix:** rewrite the model paths to `${KIPRJMOD}/JLC.3dshapes/…` and prefer the `.step` files
already present.

### H3. Silkscreen is printed onto exposed pads

The fab export (`hardware/tools/export_fab.py`) omits `--subtract-soldermask`. Re-exporting
with it changes both silk layers materially, F.Silkscreen 350,440 → 352,217 bytes, 112 diff
lines; B.Silkscreen 172,664 → 178,312, 280 diff lines. The flag adds clearance apertures that
clip silk off exposed copper.

Separately, `LED-SMD_L1.6-W0.8-R-RD.kicad_mod` places a Ø0.20 mm silk dot centred on pad 1's
corner, **on all 24 LEDs**:

```
(pad 1 smd rect (at 0.75 0.00) (size 0.800 0.800) …)      → x 0.35..1.15
(fp_circle (center 0.80 -0.40) (end 0.85 -0.40) (layer F.SilkS))
```

Marking ink is a non-wettable coating. Ink on a land is a joint that will not form.
`silk_over_copper` is set to `ignore`, so this has never been reported.

**Fix:** add `--subtract-soldermask` to the export, and move or delete the pin-1 dot in the
LED footprint.

### H4. ERC electrical checking is disarmed board-wide

Pin electrical types across the project libraries:

| Library | `unspecified` | other |
|---|---|---|
| `JLC.kicad_sym` | 85 of 95 | 6 bidirectional, 4 input |
| `ST25DV.kicad_sym` | 8 of 8 |: |

Zero `power_in`, zero `output`, zero `passive`. KiCad treats `unspecified` as compatible with
everything, so ERC on the ESP32's supply pins, the LDO output and the ST25DV's I²C lines is not
weak, it is off. A power output shorted to a power output would not fire.

The 41 `pin_to_pin` warnings currently reported all trace to this one cause.

**Fix:** type the pins on the parts that matter, supplies as `power_in`, regulator outputs as
`power_out`, I²C as `bidirectional`. This is what makes ERC mean anything.

### H5. C12 has no part number: the same failure that sent SW3 back from the fab

`fab/BOM_JLCPCB.csv`, line 51:

```
"62pF NP0 0603 (tune after VNA; 56-68pF window)",C12,C0603,select-at-order
```

The CHANGELOG describes SW3's root cause in these words: *"The BOM had the part as
'select-at-order' with a note to verify the footprint against whatever got chosen; that
verification never happened."*

The same string is in the same file today, on the NFC tuning capacitor, a value-critical part
where matching by value and package alone is exactly the wrong-part risk.

Also open: **C10's schematic `Value` is `100uF`, while its LCSC number and MPN are a 22 µF
part** (`C45783`, `CL21A226MAQNNNE`). The hand-written BOM papers over the discrepancy with a
parenthetical rather than resolving it.

**Fix:** resolve C12 before the next order. Reconcile C10's value against its part number.

### H6. Return paths are largely unreferenced

For each net, the fraction of track length with no ground copper on the opposite layer beneath it:

| Net | Length | Unreferenced |
|---|---|---|
| IMU_INT | 34.89 mm | **94.1 %** |
| SDA | 97.77 mm | 88.1 % |
| SCL | 94.82 mm | 82.2 % |
| CP4 | 136.85 mm | 75.3 % |
| +3V3 | 253.20 mm | 43.0 % |

Cause: a continuous copper-free band at roughly **y 3.4 to 5.0, x 15 to 80 on both layers**, a
~60 mm slot cut by the signal bundle feeding the LED resistor row. The top 2.5 mm edge strip of
plane is nearly detached from the body. The B.Cu plane around J1's six pads merges into a
second continuous void at x 67 to 79, y 48.6 to 51.

Return current forced around a discontinuity of this length raises loop area, ground
inductance, radiation and crosstalk together.

### H7. Every ground pad is welded solid to the plane, while the project file claims otherwise

Both zones use `(connect_pads yes (clearance 0.3))`, KiCad's **solid** mode. The
`(thermal_gap 0.5) (thermal_bridge_width 0.5)` tokens beside it are inert. Confirmed
geometrically by rasterising the fill around BT1 pad 2, C1 pad 2 and U1's thermal-pad array:
continuous copper across every pad, no annular gap, no spokes.

Meanwhile `.kicad_pro` sets `min_resolved_spokes: 2` and `starved_thermal: error`, **rules
that can never fire, because there are no spokes.** The project file reads as if thermal relief
is being policed.

For a fab-assembled all-SMD board, solid connection is defensible and arguably correct for
decoupling capacitors. The defect is the mismatch: settings that describe a policy the board
does not implement.

### H8. Exposed plane copper under the coin cell

The region under BT1 (x 52 to 72, y 18 to 38) measures **74.0 % copper on B.Cu**, the cell's own
side, and 77.8 % on F.Cu. Solder mask is the only barrier between a shifting cell and the
ground plane.

**Fix:** keep-out under the cell body on the cell's side.

## What is right, and should not be disturbed

- **SW3 is correct.** Pad-for-pad identical to KiCad's shipped `SW_SPDT_Shouhan_MSK12C02`,
  verified by diffing every `at`, `size` and `drill`. NPTH locators correct, LCSC `C431540`
  matches, 3D model resolves. It is the only footprint on the board that passes every check.
- **The gerbers match the board.** Re-plotted F.Cu, B.Cu, both silks and Edge.Cuts and compared
  by checksum ignoring comment records, identical. The 20-minute mtime gap is a checkout
  artifact, not drift.
- **No stray `.gbr` contamination** in the current `fab/`. `hardware/fab.old-jul22/` is the
  contaminated ancestor, it ships 10 forbidden documentation layers, and the current bundle
  is the fix.
- **The CPL is correct.** Header already renamed, 52 rows, mm, exact designator match with the
  BOM in both directions. `ANT1`, `J1` and `FID1 to 3` are correctly excluded.
- **Decoupling values are consistent**, 100 nF locals throughout, no mixed-decade ladder, so no
  parallel antiresonance. Bulk (132 µF) comfortably exceeds the sum of the locals, no aluminium
  electrolytic.
- **Fill type is solid** on both zones, which is current practice.
- **The front-face silkscreen artwork is deliberate.** 398 of the 414 suppressed violations are
  the neural-network illustration, and the front face carries no designators by design. The nine
  `ignore` keys were a considered decision by someone who looked, not negligence. They should
  be *scoped*, not lifted wholesale.

## Suggested order of work

1. **B1** antenna keep-out, cheapest fix, largest consequence, and the requirement is in the
   datasheet already in the repo.
2. **B2** ground islands, already reported by DRC; five stitches.
3. **B3** ESP32 decoupling, one part, one via.
4. **H1** enter real fab constraints, so DRC starts checking something.
5. **H5** resolve C12 and C10 before any order.
6. **H2** 3D model paths, one search-and-replace.
7. **H3** add `--subtract-soldermask`; fix the LED silk dot.
8. **H4** type the pins that matter.
9. **B4** power architecture, the largest question, and the one that may change the product.

Items M1, M3 are best fixed by re-drawing the affected footprints from manufacturer datasheets
rather than patching the `easyeda2kicad` output. That is the same work as fixing the root cause.
